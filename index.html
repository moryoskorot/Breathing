Before you begin

Breathing exercises affect your body, mind, and alertness. By pressing "I understand" you confirm that you:

  • Will only practise while seated or lying down in a safe environment
  • Will not practise while driving, operating machinery, or near water
  • Will stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, or unwell
  • Understand that certain exercises have explicit pregnancy, heart, and blood-pressure guidance shown on each exercise before proceeding
  • Assume full responsibility for your own safety

This app is for wellness purposes only and is not a medical device. The author makes no medical claims and accepts no liability for any injury, adverse outcome, or harm resulting from use of this application. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any new breathing practice.
