Before you begin

Breathing exercises affect your body, mind, and alertness. By pressing "I understand" you confirm that you:

  • Will only practise while seated or lying down in a safe environment
  • Will not practise while driving, operating machinery, or near water
  • Will stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, or unwell
  • Understand that certain exercises are contraindicated in pregnancy, epilepsy, cardiovascular disease, hypertension, and other conditions — check the ⚠ icon on each exercise before proceeding
  • Assume full responsibility for your own safety

This app is for wellness purposes only and is not a medical device. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any new breathing practice.
